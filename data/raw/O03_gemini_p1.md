To reduce sludge volume in a municipal wastewater treatment plant, your strategy will typically rely on high-performance **polyelectrolytes (cationic polyacrylamides as flocculants)**, **coagulants** (such as metal salts for conditioning), and sometimes advanced enzymatic or oxidation additives.

Several major chemical suppliers and manufacturers operating across Europe specialize in municipal sludge conditioning and dewatering:

### 1. Major Global & European Chemical Manufacturers

* **Kemira:** Headquartered in Finland, Kemira is one of the leading global suppliers specializing in water-intensive industries. They offer a comprehensive range of coagulants (aluminum and iron-based salts) and synthetic/organic polymers specifically designed for municipal sludge dewatering and thickener optimization.
* **SNF Group:** As one of the world's largest manufacturers of polyacrylamides, SNF has a massive presence across Europe. They provide a vast array of liquid and powder cationic polymers tailored to mechanical dewatering equipment (centrifuges, belt filter presses, and screw presses).
* **Solvay / Syensqo:** Provides specialized chemical solutions and formulations for water and wastewater treatment, focusing on solid-liquid separation and volume minimization.
* **BASF / Solenis:** Following the integration of various water treatment portfolios, Solenis (building on legacy BASF water solutions) supplies specialized flocculants, coagulants, and defoamers widely utilized in municipal sewage treatment works.

### 2. Specialized European Regional Suppliers

* **Derypol (Spain):** A dedicated European manufacturer of polymers (flocculants and coagulants) with a strong focus on sustainable water treatment technologies, offering liquid and solid polymers engineered for sludge reduction stages.
* **Separ Chemie (Germany):** A prominent regional supplier offering a wide portfolio of polymer flocculants specifically optimized for municipal sewage treatment and solid-liquid separation.
* **Nijhuis Saur Industries (NSI):** Operating widely across Europe, they provide integrated wastewater and sludge management solutions, including chemical dosing systems and process optimization chemistry.

### Key Considerations for Selection

When evaluating these suppliers for municipal applications, it is standard practice to request **jar tests and on-site rig trials** using your plant's specific sludge matrix (primary, secondary/waste activated sludge, or digested blends). Sludge dewaterability heavily depends on:

* **Charge density and molecular weight** of the polymer.
* **Rheological behavior** under high shear in centrifuges vs. low shear in gravity drainage or screw presses.
* **Carbon footprint and regulatory compliance** regarding residual monomers and European eco-labels.
