Approximately 45% of sales

## How does Kemira make money?

Kemira's business model is based on supplying essential chemicals that customers repeatedly consume in their production processes.

For example:

- A municipal wastewater plant needs a continuous supply of chemicals to treat incoming wastewater.
- A pulp mill needs chemicals throughout its pulp production and bleaching processes.
- A packaging manufacturer needs chemicals to achieve the strength and performance specifications of its paperboard.

This creates recurring demand: customers generally need ongoing deliveries, technical support and process optimization rather than making a single purchase. Kemira also works with customers to reduce chemical consumption, improve efficiency and meet stricter environmental requirements.
