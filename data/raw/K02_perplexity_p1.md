Kemira is a Finnish global chemicals company that supplies sustainable chemistry and related services to water-intensive industries—especially water treatment and fiber-based manufacturing. Its products help customers purify drinking water and wastewater, produce pulp, and make paper, board, tissue, packaging, and hygiene products more efficiently and sustainably. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/company/)

## What Kemira does

- **Water treatment:** Provides chemicals and expertise for drinking-water purification, wastewater treatment, industrial process water, sludge treatment, and water reuse. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)
- **Fiber production:** Supplies pulp-bleaching and processing chemistry, including sodium chlorate and hydrogen peroxide, to help make clean, bright, strong pulp. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/)
- **Packaging and hygiene:** Develops specialty chemicals that improve the strength, water resistance, recyclability, and performance of paper, board, tissue, molded-fiber packaging, and related products. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)
- **Industrial solutions:** Helps sectors such as food production, energy, metals, and mining manage raw water, process water, and wastewater while improving resource efficiency. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)

## Who its customers are

Kemira serves several thousand customers worldwide; its top 10 customers account for about 25% of revenue, and its top 50 for about 50%. [marketscreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/kemira-oyj-financial-statements-investor-presentation-2025-ce7e5ad2da8fff27)

| Customer group | What they buy from Kemira |

| Municipal water utilities and wastewater plants | Coagulants, polymers, and treatment solutions for drinking water and sewage; examples include cities such as Berlin, New York, Paris, and Hamburg.  [marketscreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/kemira-oyj-financial-statements-investor-presentation-2025-ce7e5ad2da8fff27) |
| Industrial water-treatment operators | Chemicals and services for process water, wastewater, and sludge management.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/business-units-markets/) |
| Pulp producers | Bleaching and pulping chemicals, especially sodium chlorate, plus process-optimization expertise.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/) |
| Paper, board, tissue, and packaging manufacturers | Specialty chemicals that improve product strength, water resistance, and production efficiency.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/business-units-markets/) |
| Mining, energy, food, and other water-intensive industries | Water-treatment and process-efficiency solutions.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/) |

In short, Kemira is largely a **business-to-business chemicals and solutions provider**: it does not sell directly to consumers, but enables utilities and manufacturers to treat water and produce fiber-based goods. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/company/)
