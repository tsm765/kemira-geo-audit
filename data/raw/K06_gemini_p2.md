Kemira’s approach to sustainability is deeply integrated into its business model as a global leader in sustainable chemical solutions for water-intensive industries (such as pulp & paper, water treatment, and energy). The company focuses on accelerating circularity, reducing its environmental footprint, maximizing its positive "handprint" (the positive impacts of its products on customers' processes), and maintaining responsible business practices.

### Core Pillars of Kemira’s Sustainability Approach

1. **Expanding in Water:** Prioritizing water management, treatment, reuse, and recycling to help societies and industries cope with water scarcity.
2. **Renewable Solutions Portfolio:** Accelerating the shift from fossil-based materials to renewable and recycled alternatives (such as bio-based polymers and barrier coatings).
3. **Climate Action & Energy Efficiency:** Grounding its climate strategy in science-based targets, increasing the use of low-carbon energy, and optimizing manufacturing efficiency.
4. **Responsible Business & Safety:** Committing to high standards in human rights, product stewardship, supply chain transparency, and workplace safety.

---

### Key Targets Set by Kemira

Kemira has established concrete, time-bound targets aligned with international frameworks like the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):

* **Greenhouse Gas Emissions (Climate Change):**
* **Scope 1 & 2:** Reduce combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by **52% by the end of 2030** (from a 2018 baseline).
* **Scope 3:** Reduce absolute Scope 3 greenhouse gas emissions across the value chain by **33% by the end of 2033** (from a 2021 baseline).
* **Net Zero Ambition:** Aiming to achieve carbon neutrality overall.


* **Renewable Offerings:**
* Derive **over EUR 500 million in revenue from renewable solutions by the end of 2030**.


* **Positive Water Impact:**
* Enable the treatment, reuse, and recycling of an **additional cumulative 3.5 billion $m^3$ of water** through customer operations by 2030 (compared to a 2024 baseline).


* **Safety & Social Standards:**
* Reduce the **Total Recordable Injury Frequency (TRIF)** rate across its sites to **1.5 by the end of 2030**.
* Reach the top 10% cross-industry norm for Diversity & Inclusion by the end of 2026.
