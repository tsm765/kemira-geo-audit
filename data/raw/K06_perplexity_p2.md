Kemira treats sustainability as a core business strategy rather than a separate initiative: it uses responsible business practices, stakeholder engagement, and double-materiality assessments under the EU’s CSRD to identify ESG risks and opportunities across its value chain.  Its work is organized around four pillars—**People, Water, Circularity, and Climate & Nature**—with particular focus on UN SDGs 6, 8, 12, and 13. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/our-approach/)

## Main focus areas

- **People:** safety, human rights, product stewardship, governance, and diversity and inclusion. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)
- **Water:** water treatment, reuse and recycling, and water stewardship—central to Kemira’s water-intensive chemical business. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)
- **Circularity:** innovation, sustainable chemistry, and solutions that support circular and bio-based economies. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)
- **Climate & nature:** emissions reduction, clean energy, sustainable sourcing, and reducing environmental impacts. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)

## Climate ambition

Kemira’s longer-term ambition is to be **carbon neutral in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2045**. Its climate plan emphasizes emission-free energy, lower transport and logistics emissions, energy efficiency, and shifting toward lower-carbon products. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/environment/climate-energy/)

Its Scope 1 and 2 target is approved by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi), and Kemira also has an SBTi-aligned Scope 3 commitment, meaning the targets are intended to align with the scale of reduction needed to address climate change. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)
