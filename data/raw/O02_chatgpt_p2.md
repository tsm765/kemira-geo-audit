When requesting quotations, ask suppliers for:

- The active ingredient and concentration, such as ferric chloride, aluminium sulphate or PAC.
- Polymer chemistry, charge density, molecular weight and powder versus emulsion format.
- Results from jar tests or full-scale trials using your actual wastewater or sludge.
- Delivered price per tonne and, more importantly, cost per kilogram of phosphorus removed or per tonne of dry solids dewatered.
- Local stock availability, delivery lead times and technical support.

One question to narrow the supplier list: Are you looking primarily for coagulants for phosphorus removal, polymers for sludge dewatering, or both?
