Kemira is a Finnish global chemicals company that supplies specialty chemicals and related services to water-intensive industries. Its core purpose is to help customers treat water, produce fiber-based materials, and improve resource efficiency. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/company/)

## What Kemira does

Kemira develops and sells chemicals that support:

- **Water treatment:** coagulants, polymers, and other treatments for drinking water, wastewater, process water, and sludge management. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)
- **Pulp production:** bleaching chemicals such as sodium chlorate and hydrogen peroxide, plus process chemicals that improve pulp quality and efficiency. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/)
- **Packaging, board, tissue, and paper:** specialty chemicals that improve strength, water resistance, printability, and recyclability of fiber-based products. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)
- **Industrial applications:** water and process-efficiency solutions for sectors including mining, energy, food production, and oil and gas. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)

In practice, Kemira’s products are often “invisible enablers”: they help make tap water safe, wastewater compliant, packaging stronger and more recyclable, and pulp production less resource-intensive. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/company/)

## Who its customers are

Kemira serves several thousand customers worldwide, with its top 10 customers accounting for roughly 25% of revenue and its top 50 for about 50%. [marketscreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/kemira-oyj-financial-statements-investor-presentation-2025-ce7e5ad2da8fff27)

| Customer group | Examples |

| Industrial water-treatment operators | Factories and facilities managing process water, wastewater, and resource efficiency.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/business-units-markets/) |

| Other water-intensive industries | Mining, energy, food production, and oil and gas companies needing water and process-chemistry solutions.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/) |

Kemira is headquartered in Helsinki, listed on Nasdaq Helsinki, and reported about EUR 2.8 billion in revenue with roughly 4,900 employees in 2025. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/company/)
