For a European municipal WWTP, the strongest suppliers to shortlist are **Kemira, SNF, Veolia Water Technologies, BASF, and Solenis**. The right choice depends less on brand and more on whether they will run site-specific jar tests and pilot trials for your sludge type and dewatering equipment. [pmarketresearch](https://pmarketresearch.com/worldwide-sludge-treatment-chemicals-market-research/)

## What to ask for

Ask each supplier to quote and trial:

- **Polymer selection:** cationic, anionic, non-ionic, dry powder, emulsion, or bead form, matched to your centrifuge, belt press, screw press, or drying bed.
- **Coagulant strategy:** ferric salts, alum, PAC, or organic coagulants—sometimes upstream coagulation improves downstream dewatering and reduces polymer demand. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/sludge-treatment-great-opportunities-for-improvement/)
- **Jar testing and full-scale pilot:** request a defined trial with baseline cake dry solids, polymer kg/t DS, filtrate/centrate TSS and phosphorus, capture rate, and disposal volume.
- **Dosing control:** sensors, automated polymer make-down/dosing, and digital optimization can reduce over-dosing and improve consistency. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/sludge-treatment-great-opportunities-for-improvement/)
- **Total cost model:** compare chemical €/t DS against transport, disposal, drying energy, and reject-water load—not polymer price alone.

## Practical shortlist

For a Finnish/European plant, I would start with **Kemira and SNF** as the primary chemical trials, add **Veolia** if you want an integrated service-and-monitoring package, and include **BASF or Solenis** to create competitive tension. Kemira’s municipal case work specifically targets drier cake, lower sludge volume, lower polymer use, and reduced reject-water load. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/cities-slash-sludge-costs-and-co2/)

A useful trial target is a **2–5 percentage-point increase in cake dry solids**, since even a small improvement can materially cut haulage and disposal volume; Kemira reports that its high-molecular-weight XD-5000 series can often deliver gains of that magnitude at lower flocculant dosage. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/cities-slash-sludge-costs-and-co2/)
