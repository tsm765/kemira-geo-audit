# Kemira’s approach to sustainability and its targets

Kemira’s sustainability strategy is built around a central idea: use chemistry to improve people’s lives and protect natural resources, while reducing the environmental impact of its own operations and value chain. The Finnish chemical company focuses particularly on water-intensive industries, including water treatment, pulp and paper, and fiber-based materials. Sustainability is integrated into its business strategy, product development, sourcing, and operations rather than treated as a separate corporate responsibility initiative.

A key distinction in Kemira’s approach is between its footprint and its handprint:

- Footprint: reducing the negative environmental and social impacts of its own operations and supply chain.
- Handprint: increasing the positive impact its products have on customers, such as enabling cleaner water, improved resource efficiency, and more sustainable materials.

The company also aligns its climate targets with the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) and links its sustainability work to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, particularly clean water, decent work, responsible consumption, and climate action.

## 3. Longer-term climate ambition

Beyond its nearer-term emissions targets, Kemira has stated an ambition to achieve carbon neutrality for Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2045. Its planned measures include investing in renewable energy, improving energy efficiency, developing lower-carbon processes and products, and working with suppliers to reduce value-chain emissions.

## 4. How sustainability supports Kemira’s business strategy

Kemira’s approach connects environmental objectives with commercial growth in three ways:

1. Expand in water: Grow its water-related business to address rising demand for clean water, wastewater treatment, and water security.
2. Build a renewable solutions portfolio: Develop and commercialize products based on renewable feedstocks and circular materials.
3. Develop new growth platforms: Apply its chemical expertise to emerging markets and applications, including more sustainable fiber-based materials.

These priorities make sustainability a driver of product innovation, customer value, and long-term revenue growth.

## Overall assessment

Kemira’s sustainability strategy combines reducing its own environmental footprint with increasing the positive impact of its products. Its main measurable priorities are cutting greenhouse gas emissions, enabling more water reuse and recycling, expanding renewable-product revenue, and improving workplace safety and inclusion.

Its 2025 results indicate progress toward the emissions goals and renewable-revenue target, while the 2030 and 2033 deadlines leave further work to do. The most distinctive aspect of its strategy is the close link between sustainability and its core business: helping customers use water, raw materials, and energy more efficiently.
