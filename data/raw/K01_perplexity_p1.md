Kemira is a Finnish global chemicals company that develops sustainable chemical solutions for water-intensive industries, especially municipal and industrial water treatment and fiber-based production. It is headquartered in Helsinki and listed on Nasdaq Helsinki under the ticker **KEMIRA**. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/company/)

## What it does

Kemira supplies chemicals and expertise that help customers treat drinking water and wastewater, improve process efficiency, and produce pulp, paper, board, tissue, packaging, and hygiene products. Its products include coagulants, polymers, process chemicals, and related digital technologies. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)

## Main business areas

Since January 2025, Kemira has organized its operations into three customer-facing segments: [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/)

- **Water Solutions** — chemicals for municipal and industrial drinking water, wastewater, raw water, and sludge treatment. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/growth-drivers/)
- **Fiber Essentials** — chemicals for pulp production and bleaching. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/)
- **Packaging & Hygiene Solutions** — chemistry for paper, board, molded-fiber packaging, tissue, textiles, and nonwovens. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)

## Company background

Founded in 1920, Kemira initially supplied fertilizers to Finnish farmers before evolving into a specialist in sustainable chemistry for water and fiber industries. In 2025, it reported revenue of about EUR 2.8 billion and employed roughly 4,900 people. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/company/)
