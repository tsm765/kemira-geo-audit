Municipal wastewater treatment facilities rely on specialized chemical manufacturers and global distributors to supply coagulants (such as metal salts and organic polymers) and flocculants (high-molecular-weight water-soluble polymers) for solid-liquid separation, clarification, and sludge dewatering.

Major global and regional suppliers in this sector include:

### 1. Major Global Manufacturers & Water Treatment Specialists

* **SNF Floerger:** A global leader specializing explicitly in water-soluble polymers, manufacturing a comprehensive line of polyacrylamide flocculants, organic coagulants (such as PolyDADMAC and polyamines), and powder/liquid polymer systems for municipal and industrial sludge dewatering.
* **Kemira Oyj:** Based in Finland, Kemira is a prominent global provider of water treatment chemistry and expertise. They supply both inorganic coagulants (aluminum and iron-based salts) and a wide portfolio of polymers tailored for municipal wastewater treatment and phosphorus removal.
* **Solenis LLC:** A global specialty chemical producer providing advanced water treatment solutions, including flocculants, coagulants, and defoamers used heavily in municipal wastewater and water-intensive industrial sectors.
* **Ecolab Inc. (Nalco Water):** Through its global industrial segment, Ecolab offers extensive water management programs, providing specialized polymer and coagulant technologies (such as their Ultrion and Nalco lines) to optimize municipal water clarification and dewatering processes.
* **BASF SE:** A massive global chemical producer offering various chemical solutions, including specialized flocculants and coagulants via its industrial solutions and chemical segments for wastewater treatment and solid-liquid separation.

### 2. Regional and Specialized Suppliers

* **Derypol:** A European manufacturer focusing on sustainable polymers (liquid and dry emulsions) and specialized technical support for municipal and industrial wastewater purification.
* **Applied Chemicals International Group (ACAT):** Operating primarily across Europe, ACAT provides specialized water treatment technologies, including synthetic and eco-friendly bio-based flocculants and coagulants, alongside chemical dosing equipment.
* **Enva:** A key environmental services and product provider offering high-activity inorganic coagulants, low-molecular-weight polymers, and polyelectrolyte flocculants (under ranges like Envirofloc) for municipal wastewater.
* **Alumichem:** A specialized supplier offering tailored coagulants and flocculants combined with customized water treatment advisory services for municipal and industrial applications.
* **Univar Solutions:** A major global chemical distributor that bridges manufacturing and municipal end-users, offering a proprietary and partnered portfolio of coagulants, flocculants (such as their CAFLON™ line), and antifoams.
