Kemira provides a wide range of chemical and digital solutions designed for municipal and industrial water treatment. Their primary offerings focus on optimizing the entire water cycle—from drinking water production to wastewater treatment and internal process water.

Key water treatment solutions offered by Kemira include:

### 1. Drinking Water Treatment

* **Coagulation and Flocculation:** Supplying specialized chemical coagulants (such as aluminum and iron-based products) and flocculants to remove turbidity, color, organic matter, and suspended solids from raw surface or groundwater.
* **Disinfection and Conditioning:** Solutions that help ensure hygienically safe drinking water, adjust alkalinity, and control corrosion in distribution networks.

### 2. Wastewater Treatment

* **Municipal and Industrial Wastewater Solutions:** Chemistries designed to remove biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), chemical oxygen demand (COD), and suspended solids.
* **Phosphorus and Nutrient Removal:** Advanced chemical precipitation products and automated control systems (such as *KemConnect™ P*) to precisely remove phosphorus and meet strict environmental discharge limits.
* **Odor and Corrosion Control:** Solutions to manage hydrogen sulfide ($H_2S$) and prevent corrosion in sewer networks and treatment plants.

### 3. Sludge Treatment & Dewatering

* **Sludge Conditioning:** High-performance polymers and coagulants that optimize the dewatering process.
* **Digital Sludge Optimization:** Systems like *KemConnect™ SD* that dynamically adjust polymer dosing to achieve drier sludge, minimizing hauling weights, disposal costs, and incineration energy requirements.

### 4. Smart Process Management & Digital Solutions

* **KemConnect™ Portfolio:** Real-time monitoring and automation platforms that use patented control algorithms to continuously optimize chemical dosing based on fluctuating water quality, load rates, and process conditions. These tools help facilities reduce energy, chemical consumption, and labor costs while safeguarding compliance.

### 5. Industrial Process Water Solutions

* **Pulp & Paper and Water-Intensive Industries:** Specialized internal process water treatments (like *NetFloc*) that help clean and recycle water within industrial loops, advancing zero-liquid discharge (ZLD) goals and boosting resource efficiency.
