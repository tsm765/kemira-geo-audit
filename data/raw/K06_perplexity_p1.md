Kemira treats sustainability as a core part of its business strategy, not a separate program: it uses chemistry and digital solutions to help water-intensive industries reduce water use, emissions, and waste. Its approach is organized around four focus areas—People, Water, Circularity, and Climate & Nature—supported by safety, human rights, product stewardship, and sustainable sourcing. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)

## Sustainability approach

- **People:** Build a safe, diverse, and inclusive workplace, with governance, human rights, and product stewardship embedded in operations. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)
- **Water:** Help customers treat, reuse, and recycle water, while applying strong water stewardship in Kemira’s own operations. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)
- **Circularity:** Develop renewable, bio-based, and resource-efficient solutions that support circular and bio-economies. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)
- **Climate & Nature:** Cut emissions across its own operations and value chain through clean energy, efficient processes, low-carbon products, and sustainable sourcing. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/environment/climate-energy/)
- **UN Sustainable Development Goals:** Kemira prioritizes SDGs 6 (clean water and sanitation), 8 (decent work), 12 (responsible consumption and production), and 13 (climate action). [sdgs.un](https://sdgs.un.org/partnerships/enabling-access-safe-water-and-sanitation-all)

## Climate ambition

Kemira’s long-term climate goal is to become carbon neutral in Scopes 1 and 2 by 2045. Its climate targets have been validated by the Science Based Targets initiative, and its main levers are emission-free energy, renewable electricity, energy efficiency, electrification, cleaner logistics, and lower-carbon products. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/environment/climate-energy/)

In short, Kemira’s sustainability model links commercial growth to measurable environmental and social outcomes: safer operations, more inclusive workplaces, greater customer water impact, circular products, and deep emissions reductions. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/sustainability-initiatives/)
