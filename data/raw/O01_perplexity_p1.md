## Market structure

Industry analyses commonly place **Kemira, Kurita, Ecolab, Solenis and Veolia** among the top five in the European water-treatment-chemicals market.  Other reports also include **BASF, SUEZ, SNF, Solvay, Nouryon and Buckman** in the leading-company set. [mordorintelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/europe-water-treatment-chemicals-market-industry)

A useful distinction:

- **Chemical manufacturers:** Kemira, SNF, BASF, Nouryon, Solvay and Italmatch.
- **Water-treatment solution providers:** Ecolab/Nalco, Solenis, Kurita and Buckman.
- **Utilities/service-led suppliers:** Veolia and SUEZ, which often bundle chemicals with plant operation, monitoring and water-management services. [mordorintelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/europe-water-treatment-chemicals-market-industry)

For a buyer in Oulu or elsewhere in Finland, **Kemira** is particularly prominent because it is Finnish-headquartered and has strong municipal, industrial and pulp-and-paper water-treatment expertise.  **SNF, Solenis, Ecolab, Veolia and SUEZ** are also relevant alternatives depending on whether the priority is bulk coagulants/flocculants, industrial programs, or outsourced water services.
