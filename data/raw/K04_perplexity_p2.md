Kemira offers end-to-end water treatment solutions for municipalities and water-intensive industries, covering drinking water, wastewater, sludge, and industrial water systems. Its approach combines treatment chemicals, technical services, equipment, and digital optimization. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)

## Core treatment areas

| Area | What Kemira provides |
|---|---|
| Raw water & drinking water | Chemicals and process support to remove particles, color, algae, and harmful bacteria, helping produce safe drinking water.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/growth-drivers/) |
| Wastewater | Treatment chemistry and process expertise for contaminant removal, phosphorus treatment, disinfection, regulatory compliance, and water reuse.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/) |
| Sludge | Sludge dewatering solutions to increase dry-solids content, reducing transport, disposal, and handling costs.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/) |
| Industrial water | Programs for boilers, cooling towers, closed loops, pretreatment, wastewater operations, and water reuse.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/products-services/industrial-water-services/) |

## Chemical portfolio

Kemira’s water-treatment chemicals include:

- Inorganic coagulants and chemical flocculants for solids, phosphorus, and organic-matter removal. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
- Antiscalants and dispersants to control scale and deposits. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
- Biocides and disinfection products for microbial control. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
- Defoamers for process control. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
- Activated carbon for contaminant removal and purification. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/industrial-solutions/)

Kemira says it is the only manufacturer offering a full portfolio of coagulants, polyacrylamide polymers, process chemicals, and other water-treatment chemicals alongside digital technologies. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/growth-drivers/)

## Digital and service solutions

- **Kemira KemConnect®**: digital services that optimize chemical dosing and treatment performance in real time. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
- **KemConnect DEX**: a chlorine-free, performic-acid-based antimicrobial/disinfection solution for wastewater, paired with automated, data-driven dosing. [marketscreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/kemira-oyj-annual-report-2025-full-ce7e5dddd880f02d)
- **Industrial water services**: on-site testing, laboratory analysis, chemical feed and monitoring equipment, Legionella management, discharge support, and continuous optimization. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/products-services/industrial-water-services/)

## Practical examples

For a municipal wastewater plant, Kemira can supply coagulants for phosphorus removal, polymers for sludge dewatering, and KemConnect DEX for disinfection. For an industrial facility, it can manage boiler and cooling-water chemistry, pretreatment, wastewater compliance, and reuse programs as a coordinated service. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
