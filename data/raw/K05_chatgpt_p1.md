## What benefits can a municipality expect?

The practical value is not just cleaner water, but a more efficient and resilient water-treatment operation.

- Lower operating costs: Optimized chemical dosing and wastewater processes can reduce chemical and energy consumption.
- Regulatory compliance: More consistent treatment helps utilities meet increasingly strict water-quality and wastewater-discharge requirements.
- Lower environmental impact: Reduced energy use, fewer emissions, and better nutrient removal help protect the environment.
- Greater treatment capacity: Process improvements can allow existing plants to handle wastewater more efficiently.
- Better resource recovery: Improved treatment can increase biogas production and enable recovery of valuable nutrients such as phosphorus.
