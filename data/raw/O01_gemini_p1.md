The European water treatment chemicals market is driven by several major global and regional players that supply coagulants, flocculants, biocides, pH adjusters, and anti-scalants for municipal and industrial applications.

The main suppliers include:

* **Kemira (Finland):** A global leader in water chemistry, Kemira specializes in chemicals for municipal and industrial water treatment, particularly known for its strong position in coagulants (such as aluminum- and iron-based products) used for drinking water and wastewater purification.
* **Ecolab / Nalco Water (USA / Global operations in Europe):** A major provider of water treatment and process chemicals, Ecolab serves various industrial sectors (including food and beverage, manufacturing, and data centers) with integrated chemical programs, digital monitoring, and automation.
* **Solenis (USA / Strong European presence):** A leading producer of specialty chemicals for water-intensive industries. Solenis expanded its footprint significantly in Europe through strategic acquisitions (such as absorbing parts of BASF's water and wastewater treatment chemical portfolios).
* **Kurita Water Industries (Japan / European HQ in Germany):** Kurita provides specialized water treatment chemicals and full-service solutions tailored for industrial facilities, power plants, and electronics manufacturing across Europe.
* **Veolia (France):** While globally recognized as an environmental services and water management giant, Veolia (via its technologies and solutions units) heavily integrates chemical treatment, engineering, and digital monitoring for large-scale municipal and industrial water cycles.
* **SNF Group (France):** One of the world’s largest manufacturers of polyacrylamides and water-soluble polymers used globally and across Europe as flocculants for wastewater treatment, mining, and sludge dewatering.
* **Univar Solutions (Global / Major European network):** A major chemical distributor that supplies core water treatment products—such as coagulants, chlorine derivatives, and pool sanitization chemicals—sourced from top chemical manufacturers to a vast network of regional clients.
