**Kemira** is a global chemical company headquartered in Helsinki, Finland, that specializes in sustainable chemical solutions for water-intensive industries.

### What Kemira Does

Kemira focuses on chemistry that improves product quality, process efficiency, and resource conservation. Its operations are structured around three main business areas:

1. **Water Solutions:** Providing chemicals and expertise for municipal and industrial water treatment, including cleaning drinking water, treating wastewater, enabling water reuse, and managing industrial water systems (such as for data centers, energy, and food and beverage facilities).
2. **Packaging & Hygiene Solutions:** Developing chemicals used in paper, board, and packaging production—focusing heavily on solutions that help reduce or replace single-use plastics, improve food-contact safety, and enhance hygiene products.
3. **Fiber Essentials:** Supplying chemicals (like bleaching and pulp-optimizing chemicals) used by the pulp and paper industry to manufacture high-quality renewable fibers, tissue, and textile raw materials.

---

### Who Are Its Customers?

Kemira primarily serves **water-intensive industries** that rely heavily on continuous resource management and process efficiency. Its core customer segments include:

* **Municipal Water Utilities:** Cities and public water treatment plants that rely on Kemira's chemicals to purify drinking water and process municipal wastewater.
* **Pulp and Paper Producers:** Manufacturers of paper, board, tissue, and fluff pulp who use Kemira's process and functional chemicals to improve production efficiency and product quality.
* **Industrial Manufacturing Sectors:** Heavy industries such as food and beverage processing, mining, energy, and commercial real estate/facilities management that require advanced industrial water treatment and wastewater recycling.
