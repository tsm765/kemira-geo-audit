The best shortlist depends on the treatment application:

- Phosphorus removal: ferric chloride, ferric sulfate, PAC or other aluminium salts.
- Sludge dewatering: cationic polyacrylamide polymers.
- Sludge thickening and clarification: polymers selected for the sludge and separation process.
- Primary or tertiary treatment: inorganic coagulants, organic coagulants and/or polymers, depending on the process design.

Product performance should be established through jar tests or full-scale trials, rather than comparing price per tonne alone.
