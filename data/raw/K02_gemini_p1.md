### What Kemira Does

Kemira provides customized chemical solutions and expertise that help industries improve their product quality, process efficiency, and resource sustainability. Its operations are divided into three main business areas:

1. **Water Solutions:** Focuses on municipal and industrial water treatment, helping to purify drinking water, treat wastewater, optimize water reuse, and manage industrial utility water systems.
2. **Packaging & Hygiene Solutions:** Provides chemistry used to manufacture paper and board packaging, reduce or replace single-use plastics, and support hygiene and food-contact applications that require strict cleanliness standards.
3. **Fiber Essentials:** Supplies chemistry essential for pulp, paper, board, and tissue production (such as bleaching chemicals and sizing agents), helping maximize the yield and recyclability of renewable natural fibers.

### Who Are Kemira's Customers?

Kemira serves businesses and entities that operate in heavy resource- and water-consuming sectors. Its primary customer base includes:

* **Municipal Water Utilities & Cities:** Public and private organizations responsible for treating drinking water and managing municipal wastewater systems.
* **Pulp, Paper, Board, and Tissue Producers:** Large-scale manufacturers that turn wood and plant fibers into paper products, cardboard, packaging materials, and tissue paper.
* **Industrial Manufacturing & Processing Plants:** Facilities across various sectors (such as food and beverage, energy, data centers, and heavy manufacturing) that require sophisticated water management, pretreatment, and wastewater recycling.
