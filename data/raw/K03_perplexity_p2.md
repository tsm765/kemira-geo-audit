## Context

Kemira operates globally, with 58 manufacturing sites, presence in 37 countries, and sales to 113 countries. Its 2025 revenue split was about 54% Europe/Middle East/Africa, 37% Americas, and 9% Asia-Pacific. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/app/uploads/2026/02/kemira-annual-review-2025.pdf)
