Kemira helps municipal water utilities make drinking-water and wastewater treatment more reliable, efficient, and compliant—mainly by supplying treatment chemicals, application expertise, and digital process-control tools. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)

## Core support

- **Treatment chemicals:** Its portfolio centers on coagulants and polymers used across the water cycle to remove impurities, separate solids, and improve water quality. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/business-units-markets/)
- **Drinking-water treatment:** Kemira’s chemistry helps utilities deliver safe, affordable drinking water while managing treatment costs. [vuosikertomukset](https://vuosikertomukset.net/resources/Kemira/fin/vuosikertomukset/Kemira_annual_report_2021.pdf)
- **Wastewater treatment:** It supports pollutant and phosphorus removal, sludge separation and dewatering, disinfection, and wastewater reuse. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)

## Efficiency and compliance

Kemira optimizes each stage of treatment to help utilities meet tightening discharge and water-quality rules while reducing spending on energy, labor, and chemicals.  For example, better chemical dosing and sludge dewatering can produce drier sludge, lower sludge volumes, and reduce hauling and disposal costs. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)

## Digital optimization

Through its KemConnect™ services, Kemira combines chemicals with automated dosing, sensors, real-time monitoring, and analytics. These tools help operators maintain compliance, optimize chemical use, save energy, and respond quickly to changing conditions. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/future-proofing-water-treatment-with-digital-technologies/)

Examples include **KemConnect™ P** for phosphorus-removal compliance, **KemConnect™ SD** for sludge-treatment optimization, and **KemConnect™ DEX**, a chlorine-free performic-acid disinfection system designed to avoid harmful disinfection by-products. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/future-proofing-water-treatment-with-digital-technologies/)

## Sustainability outcomes

Kemira also helps utilities reduce their environmental footprint by improving energy efficiency, increasing biogas production from sludge, and recovering resources such as phosphorus. In one Finnish collaboration, Joensuu’s wastewater treatment plant increased biogas output by 17%; in Copenhagen, BIOFOS reduced sludge-treatment energy use by nearly 4.4 GWh. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/data-driven-water-treatment-for-energy-gains/)
