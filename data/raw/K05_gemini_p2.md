Kemira helps municipal water utilities manage the complete water cycle—from raw and drinking water to wastewater and sludge treatment. They support municipal operations through several key areas:

* **Drinking Water Purification:** Kemira supplies specialized chemicals (such as coagulants and polymers) that remove impurities, particles, color, algae, and harmful bacteria from raw water, turning it into safe, clean drinking water for local communities.
* **Wastewater Treatment & Compliance:** They provide chemical treatments and technologies that remove organic pollutants and phosphorus from municipal wastewater. This prevents water bodies from over-fertilization (eutrophication) and helps utilities comply with tightening environmental regulations.
* **Process and Cost Optimization:** Kemira helps utilities optimize the stages of water treatment to reduce operating expenses related to energy, labor, and chemical raw materials. For example, chemical pre-treatment can improve the efficiency of biological treatment steps, cutting down overall energy use.
* **Sludge Treatment & Circular Economy:** They offer solutions for sludge dewatering and help utilities maximize biogas production from wastewater sludge, creating a non-fossil energy source that can help power local communities and advance circular economy goals.
* **Digital Technologies:** Kemira couples its chemical portfolio with smart digital monitoring and application expertise to help utilities stabilize and future-proof their continuous operations.
