Several major global chemical companies and industrial distributors specialize in supplying coagulants (such as aluminum sulfate, polyaluminum chloride, and ferric salts) and polymers (such as polyacrylamides and flocculants) for municipal wastewater treatment.

### Major Global Manufacturers & Suppliers

* **Kemira**
* **Overview:** A global leader in water chemistry, Kemira provides a comprehensive portfolio of both inorganic coagulants (aluminum and iron-based products) and organic synthetic polymers (flocculants) tailored explicitly for municipal and industrial water and wastewater treatment.


* **Solenis**
* **Overview:** Solenis offers a broad range of water treatment technologies, including high-performance flocculants, coagulants, and specialized polymers used for solids-liquid separation, sludge dewatering, and phosphorus removal in municipal plants.


* **SNF Group**
* **Overview:** As one of the world's largest manufacturers of polyacrylamide polymers, SNF supplies a massive range of liquid and powder polymers, flocculants, and coagulants designed for municipal wastewater clarification and sludge conditioning.


* **Veolia Water Technologies (KlarAid / Nalco Water / SUEZ legacy portfolios)**
* **Overview:** Veolia provides specialized lines of ready-to-use coagulants and flocculants (such as their KlarAid range) designed to optimize clarity and remove suspended solids and heavy metals in municipal facilities.


* **Ecolab (Nalco Water)**
* **Overview:** Nalco Water delivers custom-engineered coagulant and polymer programs for municipal wastewater treatment plants, focusing on optimizing clarification, improving filter performance, and enhancing sludge dewatering.

### Major Chemical Distributors

Many municipal treatment facilities source their chemical supplies through large-scale industrial chemical distributors that partner with multiple manufacturers:

* **Univar Solutions:** Supplies a wide portfolio of water treatment chemicals, including their proprietary CAFLON™ line of flocculants and coagulants, alongside inorganic salts.
* **Brenntag:** A major global chemical distributor offering a comprehensive range of municipal water treatment products, including coagulants (ferric chloride, alum, polyaluminum chloride) and synthetic polymers.
