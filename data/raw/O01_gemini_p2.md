The market for water treatment chemicals in Europe is driven by several major international and regional chemical companies, specialized manufacturers, and water technology corporations. The primary suppliers include:

### 1. **Kemira** (Finland)

* **Overview:** Kemira is one of the global and European leaders in water treatment chemicals, particularly known for its expertise in municipal and industrial water purification.
* **Key Offerings:** Coagulants and flocculants (like aluminum-based products and polymers) used for drinking water and wastewater treatment.

### 2. **Ecolab / Nalco Water** (US-headquartered with a massive European presence)

* **Overview:** Through its Nalco Water division, Ecolab is a dominant force in industrial water treatment.
* **Key Offerings:** Specialty chemicals for cooling towers, boilers, and industrial wastewater, heavily integrated with digital monitoring and automation systems.

### 3. **Solenis** (US-headquartered with strong European operations)

* **Overview:** Solenis is a major producer of specialty chemicals for water-intensive industries, expanding its footprint significantly through key acquisitions (such as acquiring certain chemical and flocculant assets from major players like BASF).
* **Key Offerings:** Water-soluble polymers, defoamers, biocides, and flocculants used in paper manufacturing, chemical processing, and municipal wastewater treatment.

### 4. **Kurita Water Industries** (Japan-headquartered with a major European hub in Germany)

* **Overview:** Kurita is a leading supplier specializing in industrial water treatment chemicals and facilities.
* **Key Offerings:** Boiler water chemicals, cooling water treatments, and ultrapure water solutions tailored for high-tech sectors like electronics, semiconductors, and power generation.

### 5. **Veolia** (France)

* **Overview:** While globally renowned as an environmental services and water management giant, Veolia (along with its specialized water technologies and solutions divisions) also produces and deploys water treatment chemicals and integrated dosing systems.
* **Key Offerings:** Comprehensive municipal and industrial water treatment packages, combining proprietary chemical programs with engineering and facility management.

### 6. **Other Key Regional and Specialty Chemical Suppliers**

* **SNF Group (France):** One of the world's largest manufacturers of polyacrylamides, heavily relied upon across Europe for water-soluble polymers used in flocculation and sludge dewatering.
* **Kuhlmann Europe:** A notable European regional producer specializing in coagulants, hydrochloric acid, caustic soda, and chlorine-based products for drinking water purification and wastewater treatment.
* **Univar Solutions:** A major chemical distributor across Europe that supplies a wide portfolio of base and specialty water treatment chemicals, including aluminum sulfate, ferric chloride, and pool sanitization agents.
