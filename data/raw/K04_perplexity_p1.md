Kemira offers chemistry-based water treatment for municipal and industrial customers, covering drinking water, wastewater, sludge, water reuse, and industrial water systems. Its approach combines treatment chemicals with digital monitoring and dosing optimization. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)

## Core chemical solutions

- **Coagulants and flocculants:** Inorganic coagulants and polyacrylamide polymers remove particles, color, algae, phosphorus, organic matter, and suspended solids from water. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
- **Phosphorus removal:** Chemical precipitation and process control help wastewater plants meet discharge limits and reduce eutrophication risks in receiving waters. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/growth-drivers/)
- **Disinfection and biocides:** Products include biocides and disinfection solutions; KemConnect™ DEX uses performic acid for rapid, chlorine-free wastewater disinfection. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
- **Antiscalants and dispersants:** These help control scale and deposits in water systems and membrane-based treatment. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
- **Defoamers and process chemicals:** Used to support stable operation across wastewater and industrial water processes. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)

## Municipal water and wastewater

Kemira supports the full urban water cycle—raw water, drinking water, wastewater, sludge, and reuse. Its chemicals help produce safe tap water, remove contaminants, improve treatment efficiency, and support regulatory compliance. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)

For wastewater plants, it offers solutions for odor and corrosion control, stormwater overflow treatment, chemically enhanced primary treatment, COD and suspended-solids removal, phosphorus removal, disinfection, and sludge dewatering. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/digital-solutions-for-resource-efficiency-in-water-treatment/)

## Digital optimization

Kemira’s **KemConnect™** services pair chemicals with sensors, automated control, and analytics to optimize dosing and process performance. Applications include phosphorus removal, sludge dewatering, disinfection, odor control, and stormwater overflow treatment. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/digital-solutions-for-resource-efficiency-in-water-treatment/)

For example, **KemConnect™ P** continuously monitors conditions and adjusts chemical phosphate dosing at multiple points, helping plants maintain phosphorus compliance despite varying loads. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/digital-solutions-for-resource-efficiency-in-water-treatment/)

## Sludge and reuse

Kemira also helps dewater sludge to higher dry-solids content, reducing transport and disposal costs, and supports water reuse and recovery to lower freshwater demand. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
