Kemira offers a comprehensive portfolio of water treatment solutions, technologies, and chemical applications designed for municipal water utilities and water-intensive industries (such as pulp and paper, oil and gas, and mining). Their offerings span across several key areas:

### 1. Drinking Water Treatment

* **Purification and Coagulation:** Providing coagulants and flocculants (such as aluminum- and iron-based products) to remove turbidity, color, organic matter, and suspended solids from surface and groundwater.
* **Disinfection and pH Adjustment:** Solutions to ensure water is safe from harmful pathogens, bacteria, and viruses while maintaining safe chemical balances and alkalinity.

### 2. Wastewater Treatment

* **Chemical Pre-treatment & Enhanced Primary Treatment:** Using specialized chemistries ahead of biological treatment stages to speed up pollutant breakdown, reduce plant energy consumption (e.g., cutting aeration energy), and double biogas production potential.
* **Phosphorus and Nutrient Removal:** Advanced chemical precipitation products and control methods to strip phosphorus and manage nitrogen levels to meet strict environmental compliance.
* **Industrial Wastewater Solutions:** Tailored treatments for heavily polluted industrial effluents, helping facilities meet zero-liquid discharge (ZLD) goals or safely discharge back into municipal systems.

### 3. Sludge Treatment & Dewatering

* **Dewatering Polymers:** Chemical solutions that bind solids together, helping municipal and industrial wastewater treatment plants achieve drier sludge volumes.
* **Volume and Cost Reduction:** Minimizing the overall volume of sludge to lower hauling, disposal, and incineration energy costs.

### 4. Smart Process Management & Digital Solutions (KemConnect™)

* **Real-Time Optimization:** Combining chemical expertise with automation and patented control algorithms.
* **Targeted Monitoring:** Digital platforms like *KemConnect™ P* (for precision phosphorus removal) and *KemConnect™ SD* (for smart sludge dewatering) continuously monitor process conditions and adjust chemical dosing automatically to maximize efficiency and cut down on waste and operational costs.

### 5. Internal Process Water (Pulp & Paper)

* **NetFloc and Closed-Loop Systems:** Solutions designed for pulp and paper mills to treat and recycle internal process water, improving resource efficiency and supporting circular economy practices.
