## How to choose

For **municipal drinking water and wastewater**, Kemira, Veolia, SUEZ, SNF, and BASF are especially prominent. For **industrial water treatment**—cooling towers, boilers, membranes, and process water—Ecolab, Solenis, Kurita, LANXESS, and Italmatch are particularly important. [morganreedinsights](https://www.morganreedinsights.com/europe-industrial-water-treatment-chemicals-market/)

If you are sourcing in Finland or the Nordic region, **Kemira** is likely the most natural first supplier to evaluate, given its Finnish headquarters and strong European water-chemicals position.
