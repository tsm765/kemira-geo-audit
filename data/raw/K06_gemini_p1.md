Kemira’s approach to sustainability is deeply integrated into its business model as a global leader in sustainable chemical solutions for water-intensive industries (such as pulp & paper, wastewater treatment, and energy). The company focuses on addressing global megatrends like climate change, water scarcity, and the responsible use of limited resources.

### Kemira’s Core Approach

1. **Growing the "Handprint":** Delivering chemistry with a purpose—providing products and digital services that help customers optimize resource efficiency, improve water treatment, and transition away from fossil-based materials.
2. **Reducing the "Footprint":** Minimizing the environmental impact of its own operations and supply chain through renewable/recycled raw materials, energy efficiency, and ambitious climate targets.
3. **Responsible Business Practices:** Operating with transparency, adhering to international frameworks like the UN Global Compact (and its CEO Water Mandate), the UN Forward Faster initiative, and conducting double-materiality assessments under the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD).

---

### Key Sustainability Targets

Kemira has established validated, science-backed targets and operational goals across climate, water, circularity, and social performance:

#### 1. Climate & Emissions (Science-Based Targets initiative - SBTi)

* **Scope 1 & 2 Emissions:** Reduce combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions by **52% by the end of 2030** (from a 2018 baseline).
* **Scope 3 Emissions:** Reduce absolute Scope 3 GHG emissions across the value chain by **33% by the end of 2033** (from a 2021 baseline).
* **Net-Zero Ambition:** Target to achieve carbon neutrality/net-zero.

#### 2. Renewable Solutions & Portfolio Growth

* **Renewable Revenue:** Target over **EUR 500 million in revenue from renewable solutions by the end of 2030** (up from earlier baseline figures, scaling up bio-based and circular alternatives).

#### 3. Water Stewardship & Positive Impact

* **Water Impact:** Enable the treatment, reuse, and recycling of an additional cumulative **3.5 billion $m^3$ of water** through customer operations by 2030 (compared to a 2024 baseline).

#### 4. People, Safety, and Inclusion

* **Safety:** Reduce the Total Recordable Injury Frequency (TRIF) rate across all sites to **1.5 by the end of 2030**.
* **Diversity & Inclusion:** Reach the top 10% cross-industry norm for Diversity & Inclusion by the end of 2026.
