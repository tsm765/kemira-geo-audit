Kemira is a Finnish global chemicals company that develops sustainable chemical solutions for water-intensive industries, especially water treatment and fiber-based products. It is headquartered in Helsinki and listed on Nasdaq Helsinki under the ticker **KEMIRA**. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/company/)

## What it does

Kemira supplies chemicals and expertise that help make drinking water safe, treat wastewater, and improve processes in pulp, paper, packaging, hygiene, food, energy, and mining industries. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)

Its main business areas are:

- **Water Solutions** — coagulants, polymers, and process chemicals for municipal and industrial drinking water, wastewater, and sludge treatment. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/growth-drivers/)
- **Fiber Essentials** — chemicals for pulp production, bleaching, and fiber processing. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/)
- **Packaging & Hygiene Solutions** — chemistry for paper, board, molded-fiber packaging, tissue, textiles, and nonwovens. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/)
