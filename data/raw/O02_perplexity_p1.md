## What they typically supply

- **Inorganic coagulants:** alum, ferric chloride, ferric sulfate, PAC, and aluminum chlorohydrate.
- **Organic coagulants:** cationic polymers used to neutralize particle charges.
- **Flocculant polymers:** anionic, cationic, or nonionic polyacrylamides in powder, emulsion, or solution form.
- **Sludge-conditioning chemicals:** polymers and blends that improve thickening, centrifuge or belt-press dewatering, and cake dryness. [chemicalresearchinsight](https://chemicalresearchinsight.com/2026/02/24/top-10-companies-in-the-global-sludge-conditioning-chemical-market-2024-innovations-driving-sustainable-water-treatment/)
