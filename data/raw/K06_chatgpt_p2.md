# Kemira’s approach to sustainability and its targets

Kemira’s approach to sustainability is to reduce the negative environmental and social impacts of its own operations and value chain while maximizing the positive impact of its chemical solutions for customers and society. The Finnish chemical company focuses particularly on clean water, circularity, renewable materials, climate action and responsible business practices.

Sustainability is embedded in Kemira’s business strategy, which aims to expand its water business, grow its portfolio of renewable solutions and develop new growth opportunities. Its solutions help water-intensive industries improve resource efficiency, treat and reuse water, and reduce their reliance on fossil-based raw materials.

## 4. How does Kemira ensure accountability?

Kemira supports its sustainability strategy with several governance and reporting mechanisms:

- Science-based climate targets: Its near-term greenhouse gas reduction targets have been validated by the SBTi.
- Sustainability reporting: Its 2025 Sustainability Statement follows the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) under the EU sustainability reporting framework.
- Responsible business and sourcing: Its Code of Conduct, human-rights commitments, product stewardship policies and supplier sustainability assessments extend its approach across the value chain.
- External assessments: Kemira reports ratings including EcoVadis Gold, an MSCI ESG rating of AAA, and CDP ratings of A− for both climate and water security. These ratings reflect different assessment methodologies and should not be treated as interchangeable.

## Overall assessment

Kemira’s sustainability strategy combines environmental impact reduction with business-led sustainability growth. Rather than treating sustainability solely as regulatory compliance, it seeks to make clean water, circular materials, resource efficiency and lower-carbon products central to its competitive position.

Its most distinctive commitments are the substantial increase in water treatment and reuse enabled through customers, the emissions reductions aligned with climate science, and the goal of generating more than €500 million from renewable solutions by 2030. Together, these targets connect Kemira’s environmental responsibilities with its long-term business strategy.
