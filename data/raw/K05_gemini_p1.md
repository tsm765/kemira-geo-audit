Kemira helps municipal water utilities manage the complete water cycle—from raw water intake to drinking water production, wastewater treatment, and sludge handling. Their solutions focus on ensuring compliance with strict environmental regulations, optimizing operational costs, and improving overall sustainability.

Here is how Kemira supports municipal water utilities across different stages:

### 1. Drinking Water Production (Raw Water Treatment)

* **Impurity Removal:** Kemira provides specialized chemicals (such as coagulants and polymers) that remove suspended solids, natural organic matter, color, algae, and harmful bacteria from raw surface or groundwater sources.
* **Safety and Affordability:** Their treatment solutions help utilities consistently deliver safe, clean, and high-quality tap water that meets public health standards.

### 2. Wastewater Treatment & Compliance

* **Pollutant Removal:** Chemical treatments prevent organic and inorganic impurities, heavy metals, and nutrients (like phosphorus) from entering natural water bodies, helping to avoid issues like overfertilization (eutrophication) and biodiversity loss.
* **Regulatory Compliance:** As environmental standards tighten globally, Kemira’s portfolio helps municipal plants stay compliant with stricter regulations regarding discharge limits.

### 3. Energy and Cost Optimization

* **Energy Reduction:** Wastewater treatment is notoriously energy-intensive, particularly during biological treatment phases. Kemira uses chemical pre-treatment methods to speed up the treatment process, reducing the energy load on biological systems and lowering overall carbon footprints.
* **Operational Efficiency:** Their customized chemistry and dosing optimization help utilities reduce expenses related to energy, labor, and raw materials.

### 4. Sludge Treatment and Circular Economy

* **Sludge Dewatering:** Kemira supplies polymers that optimize sludge dewatering (removing water from the leftover solid waste), which significantly reduces the volume, handling, and disposal costs of wastewater sludge.
* **Resource Recovery & Energy Generation:** They help utilities pioneer technologies for biogas production from wastewater sludge, converting waste into a non-fossil energy source that can power local communities.

### 5. Digital Technologies

* **Smart Water Management:** Alongside chemistry, Kemira offers digital tools and monitoring technologies that give utilities real-time insights into their treatment processes, allowing for better process control and predictive maintenance.
