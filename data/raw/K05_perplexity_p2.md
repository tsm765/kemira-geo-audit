Kemira helps municipal water utilities make drinking-water and wastewater treatment more reliable, compliant, and cost-efficient—mainly by combining treatment chemicals with process expertise and digital optimization. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)

## What it provides

- **Treatment chemicals:** Coagulants and polymers for clarifying water, removing pollutants, and improving sludge handling are central to Kemira’s water-treatment portfolio. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/business-units-markets/)
- **Process support:** Its experts review plant processes and technologies, helping utilities meet water-quality targets and tightening regulations. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
- **Digital services:** KemConnect tools provide real-time monitoring, automated chemical dosing, and process feedback for applications such as phosphorus removal, sludge dewatering, and disinfection. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/future-proofing-water-treatment-with-digital-technologies/)

## Practical outcomes

| Utility challenge | Kemira’s contribution |
|---|---|
| Meeting discharge limits | Automated phosphorus-removal control helps keep total phosphorus consistently low and supports permit compliance.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/future-proofing-water-treatment-with-digital-technologies/) |
| High energy and chemical costs | Optimized dosing and sludge treatment can reduce chemical use, energy consumption, and sludge volumes.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/future-proofing-water-treatment-with-digital-technologies/) |
| Sludge management | Better dewatering produces drier sludge, lowering hauling and disposal costs while making biogas and nutrient recovery more viable.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/future-proofing-water-treatment-with-digital-technologies/) |
| Disinfection | KemConnect DEX uses performic acid with automated dosing, avoiding chlorine-related harmful by-products.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/future-proofing-water-treatment-with-digital-technologies/) |
| Climate and resource goals | Digitalized treatment supports lower carbon footprints and more efficient use of water, energy, and chemicals.  [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/future-proofing-water-treatment-with-digital-technologies/) |

## Examples

Kemira reports that a collaboration with Joensuu, Finland increased biogas output by 17%, while Copenhagen’s BIOFOS reduced sludge-treatment energy use by nearly 4.4 GWh through an optimized dewatering polymer and KemConnect SD.  In Prague, Veolia used KemConnect P to improve phosphorus control and cut phosphorus-precipitation costs on one water line by almost 34%. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/stories/data-driven-water-treatment-for-energy-gains/)

In short, Kemira acts less like a simple chemical supplier and more like a treatment partner: it supplies the chemistry, helps tune the process, and adds digital controls so utilities can deliver safe water while controlling costs and environmental impact. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/water/)
