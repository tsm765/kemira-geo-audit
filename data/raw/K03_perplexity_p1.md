Kemira is a large Finnish specialty-chemicals company: in 2025 it had revenue of **EUR 2.75 billion** and **4,911 employees** at year-end. Its shares trade on **Nasdaq Helsinki** under the ticker **KEMIRA**. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/app/uploads/2026/02/kemira-annual-review-2025.pdf)

## Stock listing

Kemira Oyj is listed on **Nasdaq Helsinki** (the Helsinki Stock Exchange), with the share symbol **KEMIRA**. It has been listed there since 1994. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/investors/kemira-as-an-investment/)

For context, its 2025 revenue split was roughly EUR 1.49 billion in Europe, Middle East and Africa, EUR 1.01 billion in the Americas, and EUR 247 million in Asia-Pacific. [kemira](https://www.kemira.com/app/uploads/2026/02/kemira-annual-review-2025.pdf)
